# Task-2-DimzeeDuff
Repository for Task-2

This project involved performing Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA) on a 1,200-row e-commerce customer orders dataset to uncover patterns, trends, and outliers using Microsoft Excel.

Records: 1,200 orders

Columns: 14 (OrderID, Date, Product, Quantity, UnitPrice, TotalPrice, PaymentMethod, OrderStatus, ReferralSource, CouponCode, and more)

Date Range: January 2023 – June 2025

What Was Done

Calculated basic statistics — mean, median, min, max, and standard deviation across key numeric columns

Analysed product performance by revenue and order volume

Broke down order status distribution across all 1,200 records

Compared revenue and average spend across payment methods

Evaluated acquisition channel performance by referral source

Tracked monthly revenue trends across 30 months

Identified 8 statistical outliers using the IQR method

Key Findings

Total revenue: $1,264,761.96

Average order value: $1,053.97

41.4% of orders are Cancelled or Returned

Instagram is the top acquisition channel at $275,285 in revenue

Chair and Printer lead in product revenue at ~$195K each

June 2024 was the peak revenue month at $68,068

Credit Card users have the highest average spend at $1,127 per order

FREESHIP coupon drives the most revenue at $335K

Tools Used

Microsoft Excel
